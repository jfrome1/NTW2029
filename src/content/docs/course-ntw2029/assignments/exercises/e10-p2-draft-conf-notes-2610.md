## Purpose

This exercise helps you consolidate guidance from my recorded feedback and our conference on your Paper 2 full draft. By articulating what you learned, you'll better internalize key revisions and develop a clear strategy for your Paper 2 final draft.

## Overview

To capture the key insights and guidance from my recorded feedback and our conference, answer the following questions about the feedback and your revision plans for Paper 2.

## Requirements

Copy and paste the questions below into a document and type your answer below each question, in full sentences. Keep the questions numbered:

1. **What were the 2 or 3 most important points of feedback on your draft?** Include points from my recorded feedback as well as from our conference, and be specific about what needed clarification, revision, or rethinking.

2. **What other points of feedback did we discuss, or did I raise in my recorded feedback?**

3. **What are your planned next steps for revising your draft?** Focus on the most important changes, and describe each one specifically.

4. **Is there anything about the target article or the commentary that you still don't understand?** If so, describe each point you still need to clarify.

5. **(Optional)** Do you have any other comments or feedback for me?

Include a word count at the top of your document.

## Guidelines

- Be specific when describing feedback points: refer to particular paragraphs, claims, or quotations in your draft
- Your answer to question 3 should focus on the most important planned changes (you don't need to address every point of feedback), but don't be vague, and formulate an actual, specific plan (e.g. not _I plan to explain the disagreement more clearly_ but _I plan to quote the commentary's description of the target's claim and compare it with what the target actually says_)
- Since it's hard to remember everything, I encourage you to rewatch my recorded feedback and review the conference transcript while answering these questions

### Getting help

- Don't ask me questions in this assignment; if you want me to answer, email or message me separately
- After this conference, you may ask me [specific questions](/course-ntw2029/course-info/conferences#what-counts-as-a-specific-question), but [I can't review emailed draft sections](/course-ntw2029/course-info/conferences#can-i-email-you-drafts-or-revisions-for-additional-feedback).

## Feedback

This exercise won't receive individual comments but will inform my understanding of your writing development process (15% of final grade).
