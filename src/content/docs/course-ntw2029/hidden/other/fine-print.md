NTW2029 website fine print changes and additions

## GenAI

- Students are responsible not just for reporting generative AI use but for keeping copies of work and accessible versions of work in development in order to provide evidence of appropriate AI use. Such evidence includes all course-related chat logs. Failure to retain evidence of course-related activities may be interpreted as evidence that AI non-use claims are inaccurate. It is your responsibility to keep and track whatever you need to demonstrate that your AI use claims are true.
- No GenAI use means no GenAI use either directly or indirectly, either by you or by anyone you interact with about your course assignments. If you interact with others about your coursework (such as asking them for feedback on potential submissions), you must provide a detailed explanation of the course policy and have them agree to not use GenAI on your paper unless they follow the same policy requirements as enrolled students. You will be considered responsible for all GenAI use on your work, even if you did not authorize or request it.
- A condition of earning credit is that you can show both intellectual ownership of the work you submit and that you can explain, with specific examples from your own paper, moments of the writing process that reflect the guidance given in the course about how to write a paper. If you are unable to demonstrate both of these things, you will not receive credit for the assignment in question. That zero credit is not a penalty for academic dishonesty; it is a grading criterion that must be met to earn credit through the work, and it applies whether or not any GenAI use is established.
- I may ask you to explain or account for any part of your submitted work at any time, including in a meeting arranged for that purpose outside the scheduled conferences.
- A share link shows the conversation as it existed at the moment you created the link. You are responsible for each link you submit showing the entire conversation it covers. A link that shows only part of the actual chat is considered an incomplete disclosure.

## Submitting work and late penalties

- The late assignment policy summarized as "up to one letter grade per day late" should be read to mean "up to one letter grade penalty applied at 1 min late and an additional grade penalty of up to one letter grade applied after each additional 24-hour day"

## Penalties are preliminary and not finalized until final grades are submitted

- Any decisions about consequences for course policy violations or for failing to meet assignment standards (including grade penalty determinations) should be considered preliminary until the end of the term. Consequences may be reassessed, even after communicating them to the student, up to the maximum allowable under the course policy, based on a holistic assessment of relevant circumstances.

## Collection of course materials

I collect the following information and/or record the following events during the course:

- all conversations with students, including conferences
- all class meetings
- student AI chats
- student writing in Google Docs
- submitted work
- emails and other messages

I may use collected written material, including transcripts of recordings in the following ways:

- for improvement of the course
- as inputs for assignment assessment
- as a record of our interactions
- in future term course materials (with names and identifying information removed)
- as evidence in cases of potential academic dishonesty or policy violations

You may opt out of the above uses with a written request made anytime before final grades are released. Due to the difficulty of removing anonymized student information from data sets, I can only guarantee honoring such requests if they occur before final grades are released.

## Other

- Any disagreement with any course policies should be raised proactively before failure to meet those policies. Continued enrollment in the course shall be considered agreement to both the letter and spirit of such policies. Students are voluntarily accepting the stated consequences if they choose to not comply with the policies.
