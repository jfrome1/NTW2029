If you wrote your assignment in a Google Doc, you share your writing process by giving me edit access to that document. Edit access also lets me open the document's full version history, which is what shows me how your writing developed.

The steps below show you how to give me edit access and how to copy the link you paste into your submission.

## Step 1. Open the Google Doc

Open the Google Doc you wrote the assignment in.

## Step 2. Click the "Share" button

With the document open, click the "Share" button in the top right corner. The sharing dialog opens, and it is where you choose who can open the document and what they can do with it.

![A Google Doc with the Share button in the top right corner](/images/gdocs-share-button.png)

## Step 3. Add my Gmail address

In the "Add people, groups, and calendar events" box, type jonathan.frome@gmail.com. Use that address and not my NUS address.

<!-- Screenshot needed: the share dialog with jonathan.frome@gmail.com typed into the add-people box -->

## Step 4. Change Viewer to Editor

Google gives every person you add Viewer access to start with, so the dropdown next to my address already says "Viewer" once you have added me. Click that dropdown, choose "Editor", and then click "Send". Viewer access is not enough, because Editor access is what lets me open the version history.

<!-- Screenshot needed: the role dropdown next to my address, open, with Editor about to be selected -->

## Step 5. Copy the link

Click "Copy link" in the sharing dialog, then paste the link at the top of your submission alongside your chat links. If the dialog closed when you clicked "Send", click "Share" again to reopen it.

## Do not use "Anyone with the link"

The sharing dialog also has a General access section, where "Anyone with the link" gives access to everyone on the internet who has the link. You do not need it for this course, and it shares your writing more widely than the course asks. Adding my address gives access to me alone.
